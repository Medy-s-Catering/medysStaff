System Features

Everything Staff Need

A complete digital toolkit designed specifically for Medy's Catering's day-to-day operations.

Booking Management

Create, view, edit, and delete client event bookings. Filter by status, package, and date with instant search.

Event Schedule Calendar

Interactive monthly calendar with color-coded events by type. Toggle between calendar and list views.

Client Feedback

Record and manage client feedback with star ratings. Track unread responses and monitor satisfaction scores.

Reports & Analytics

Visual booking charts, status breakdowns, event type analytics, and package popularity — all printable.

Staff Account Management

Admin-only control to create, edit, and manage staff accounts with role-based access permissions.

Live Dashboard

At-a-glance summary of bookings, pending approvals, recent feedback, and upcoming events on login.

How It Works

Simple. Fast. Organized.

1
Log In to the Portal

Staff and administrators log in using their assigned credentials. Access is granted based on role — Admin or Staff.

2
Manage Bookings

When a client books an event, staff encode the details — event type, date, venue, guest count, and package preference.

3
Track on the Schedule

All confirmed bookings appear on the event calendar. Staff can view booked dates and avoid scheduling conflicts.

4
Collect Feedback & Generate Reports

After each event, staff record client feedback. Admins review reports to track performance and improve service quality.
